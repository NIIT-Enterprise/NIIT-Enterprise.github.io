Programme Overview

Forward Deployed Engineer

FORGE — the FDE Readiness Program — prepares experienced engineers to operate as Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) on customer-embedded Retail and CPG pods. An FDE works beyond the traditional engineering seat — sitting with the customer, framing ambiguous commercial problems, and driving solutions from discovery through to production-grade delivery.

The programme is a single capability system rather than a set of standalone courses. It is organised along two axes — experience level and technical capability — and built on one continuous Retail/CPG engagement that runs from the foundation through the specialist bootcamp and into a supervised pod project. Level 1 (2–5 years) develops offshore execution depth; Level 2 (5–8 years) develops onsite-capable workstream ownership. Each level runs in two capabilities — Data Engineering and Agentic AI — giving four specialist tracks that sit on top of one Common Foundation.

Every module is deliberately hands-on: engineers build, test, break and operate real Retail/CPG workloads, and are certified individually on named work packages rather than on team output. A Shared Delivery and Scale Annex carries the delivery model, engagement case, pod simulation, assessment rubric and readiness standard that all five designs rely on.

Common FDE Foundation

Retail / CPG Customer Delivery and Pod Readiness
Common FoundationBoth levels · 2–8 yrsGated entry
8 hrs guided pre-work + 16-hour intensive foundation, instructor-led on a preconfigured Retail/CPG sandbox. Cohort size 20–25 with one lead mentor and one lab mentor. Outputs feed directly into the specialist bootcamp.
Programme Introduction

The Common Foundation sets the operating baseline that every Forward Deployed Engineer needs before specialising. Its purpose is narrow and deliberate: to convert engineers who are used to receiving specified work into engineers who can sit in front of an ambiguous Retail or CPG business problem, frame it, size it, and take the first slice of it to something demonstrable.

It is grounded in a single client engagement that continues through the specialist bootcamp and the supervised project, so nothing produced here is thrown away. The discovery brief, KPI map, architecture hypothesis and estimate created in these sixteen hours become the starting backlog for whichever specialist track the participant enters.

Consulting capability is treated as an engineering competency rather than a soft skill. Module 4 teaches it explicitly — how to set expectations with a US-based stakeholder, how to renegotiate scope without losing credibility, when to escalate and how — and every subsequent module puts it under observation.

Who should be nominated
  • Technical professionals with 2–8 years of experience identified for FDE pod deployment.
  • Data engineers, software engineers, AI/ML engineers and technical analysts moving into customer-embedded delivery.
  • Individuals with a realistic prospect of being staffed on a customer pod within 90 days of completion.
Entry guardrails
  • Working proficiency in Python and SQL, evidenced through the placement diagnostic.
  • Practical familiarity with Git, REST APIs, relational databases and at least one cloud platform.
  • Ability to complete hands-on labs independently within a timed session.
  • Confirmed release of time for the supervised project window.
Key Outcomes

On completion of the foundation, a participant can:

  • Run a structured customer discovery conversation on an ambiguous Retail/CPG problem without turning it into a requirements interview.
  • Decompose that problem into a KPI tree, a root-cause hypothesis and an MVP boundary with measurable success criteria.
  • Produce a defensible data or agent architecture hypothesis and use AI coding assistance to build a bounded vertical slice from it.
  • Explain an engineering trade-off in business language to a non-technical stakeholder and hold a position under challenge.
  • Operate the onsite/offshore working cadence: async handover, decision logging, early escalation and US-hours playback.
  • Build and defend a three-month engagement estimate covering scope, staffing, dependencies, milestones and risk.

GATE Foundation Gate — entry to a specialist track ▾

Entry to a specialist track is gated, not automatic. The gate is a 30-minute panel review of four artefacts:

  • Discovery brief with a defensible problem statement and named success criteria.
  • Retail/CPG KPI map linking the business problem to measurable commercial outcomes.
  • Architecture hypothesis with stated assumptions, risks and a thin-slice boundary.
  • Three-month engagement estimate the participant can defend under challenge.

Participants who do not clear the gate join a four-hour clinic and re-present. The gate result is recorded on the readiness dashboard and informs the eventual billable-ready decision.

Level 1 Data Engineering FDE

2–5 Years | Offshore Execution Depth | Retail / CPG
Data EngineeringLevel 1 · 2–5 yrsOffshore build seat
8 hrs pre-work + 16-hr Common Foundation + 32-hr specialist bootcamp + 6-week supervised pod project at 4–6 hrs/week, with a weekly two-hour mentor clinic. Total learner commitment 80–92 hours.
Programme Introduction

This track builds data engineers who can be handed a bounded work package on a live Retail or CPG engagement and take it to production quality without close supervision. The emphasis is implementation, debugging and operability rather than architecture theory — the participant is being prepared for the offshore build seat in the pod, where the measure of value is clean, tested, observable work that lands on time and hands over cleanly.

The engineering is deliberately opinionated about Retail/CPG data. Participants work with the awkward realities: SKU hierarchies that change mid-quarter, POS feeds that arrive late and duplicated, syndicated market data on a different grain to internal sales, and promotion data that only makes sense with the retailer agreement beside it. Two applied project tracks — Knowledge Assist and Contract Intelligence — take the data platform through to an AI-consumable layer.

Who should be nominated
  • Data engineers and software engineers with 2–5 years of experience.
  • Engineers expected to contribute to migration, modernisation and analytics-platform engagements from offshore.
Entry guardrails
  • Working proficiency in Python and SQL, evidenced through the placement diagnostic.
  • Practical exposure to at least one cloud platform.
  • Familiarity with ETL/ELT concepts, Git and REST APIs.
  • Successful completion of the Common FDE Foundation gate.
Key Outcomes

On completion, a participant can:

  • Profile fragmented Retail/CPG sources and express them as enforceable data contracts with privacy classification.
  • Build incremental batch pipelines that survive late, duplicate and out-of-order data without manual intervention.
  • Implement Kafka-based event processing with correct keying, partitioning, windowing and dead-letter handling.
  • Deliver curated, tested and documented data products suitable for commercial analytics and AI consumption.
  • Build the data layer beneath a knowledge assistant and a contract intelligence workflow.
  • Deploy, instrument, break and recover a pipeline, and write the runbook a support team can actually use.
  • Use an AI coding assistant productively and defend every change it produced in review.
Target Role in the Pod

Offshore execution depth. Level 1 participants own bounded work packages end to end: they build, test, deploy, resolve their own defects, produce complete handovers at India close, and escalate risk early enough for the onsite lead to act on it. They are not expected to set architecture, but they are expected to challenge it when the implementation tells them something the design did not anticipate.

Level 1 Agentic AI FDE

2–5 Years | Offshore Execution Depth | Retail / CPG
Agentic AILevel 1 · 2–5 yrsOffshore build seat
8 hrs pre-work + 16-hr Common Foundation + 32-hr specialist bootcamp + 6-week supervised pod project at 4–6 hrs/week, with a weekly two-hour mentor clinic. Total learner commitment 80–92 hours.
Programme Introduction

This track builds engineers who can deliver a bounded agent work package that is reliable, grounded and supportable rather than merely impressive in a demo. The distinction matters commercially: most agent prototypes fail at the point where a customer asks what happens when the retrieval is wrong, the tool times out, or the user says something the flow did not anticipate. Every lab in this track is built around those questions.

The work is grounded in Retail and CPG operations — a category manager asking what the agreed listing terms are for a retailer, a field team querying stock and promotion status mid-conversation, a partner-support flow that has to escalate cleanly to a human. Participants build a single agent well before touching orchestration, and every build carries evaluation, guardrails and tracing from the first commit.

Who should be nominated
  • Software, data and AI engineers with 2–5 years of experience.
  • Developers expected to implement agent workflows inside a pod under senior technical direction.
Entry guardrails
  • Working proficiency in Python, evidenced through the placement diagnostic.
  • Practical experience with REST APIs, Git and at least one cloud platform.
  • Basic familiarity with LLM prompting and structured outputs.
  • Successful completion of the Common FDE Foundation gate.
Key Outcomes

On completion, a participant can:

  • Engineer prompts and context pipelines that produce schema-valid, testable outputs under regression.
  • Build a RAG assistant over Retail/CPG documents and report measured retrieval and groundedness metrics.
  • Implement a multi-turn conversation with explicit state, disambiguation, fallback and human escalation.
  • Integrate enterprise tools with safe contracts, idempotency, timeouts and approval for high-risk actions.
  • Build an evaluation suite covering functional, adversarial, injection and regression cases.
  • Deploy an agent service with tracing, cost and latency controls, and recover it from injected failure.
  • Use an AI coding assistant productively and defend every change it produced in review.
Target Role in the Pod

Offshore execution depth. Level 1 participants own bounded agent work packages: a retrieval layer, a conversation flow, a tool integration, an evaluation suite. They are accountable for the reliability of what they build, for evidence that it works, and for handing it over in a state a colleague in another time zone can operate.

Level 2 Data Engineering FDE

5–8 Years | Onsite-Capable Workstream Owner | Retail / CPG
Data EngineeringLevel 2 · 5–8 yrsOnsite seat
8 hrs pre-work + 16-hr Common Foundation + 40-hr specialist bootcamp + 8-week supervised pod project at 6–8 hrs/week, with a weekly two-hour mentor clinic. Total learner commitment 112–128 hours.
Programme Introduction

This track prepares experienced data engineers to hold the onsite seat: to own a customer-facing data workstream, make architecture decisions in the room, decompose the work for an offshore pod, and defend the estimate and the trade-offs when the customer changes their mind in week eight. The participant remains hands-on throughout — every architectural claim in this design has to be evidenced in working code, measured performance or a survived failure drill.

The Retail/CPG content is deeper than at Level 1 and more concerned with identity and semantics: reconciling customer and SKU identity across channels, harmonising syndicated market data against internal sales at a different grain, and building a commercial semantic layer that a category team and a demand planner can both trust. The three applied areas are then owned at architecture level rather than implemented as bounded packages.

Who should be nominated
  • Senior data engineers, lead engineers and aspiring data platform leads with 5–8 years of experience.
  • Engineers being positioned for the onsite or workstream-owner seat in a customer pod.
Entry guardrails
  • Advanced Python and SQL with production ownership history.
  • Production experience on at least one cloud data platform.
  • Working experience of CI/CD, distributed systems and operational debugging.
  • Successful completion of the Common FDE Foundation gate.
Key Outcomes

On completion, a participant can:

  • Lead discovery on a fragmented Retail/CPG data estate and produce a defensible target architecture with stated trade-offs.
  • Design and implement scalable batch, CDC and streaming architectures with reconciliation and replay.
  • Build a governed commercial semantic layer that resolves customer and SKU identity across channels.
  • Own the data architecture beneath knowledge assist, conversational AI and contract intelligence workloads.
  • Engineer platform reliability: SLOs, error budgets, incident command, disaster recovery and FinOps.
  • Decompose a three-month workstream, staff it, sequence dependencies and defend the estimate under change.
  • Set and govern AI-assisted engineering practice for an offshore pod.
Target Role in the Pod

Onsite-capable workstream owner. Level 2 participants shape the solution, decompose it into work packages the offshore team can execute, run the customer conversation, absorb scope change without destabilising the pod, and remain personally accountable for architecture, estimate and business outcome.

Programme Introduction

This track prepares experienced AI and software engineers to own an agentic solution workstream from discovery through production operation. The hardest judgement in this space is not technical construction but boundary setting: deciding what should be an agent at all, where determinism must wrap probabilistic reasoning, what a tool is allowed to do without a human, and how much evaluation is enough before a customer-facing release. Those judgements are the spine of this design.

Participants build all three Retail/CPG applied areas and then orchestrate them into a single coherent solution, which is where multi-agent design earns or fails to earn its complexity. Architecture positions are not accepted on assertion — each is defended with comparative metrics on quality, latency and token cost from the participant’s own implementations.

Who should be nominated
  • Senior AI/ML engineers, GenAI engineers, lead developers and platform engineers with 5–8 years of experience.
  • Engineers being positioned for the onsite or workstream-owner seat in a customer pod.
Entry guardrails
  • Strong Python and software engineering fundamentals with production ownership history.
  • Experience with APIs, cloud services, CI/CD and running production systems.
  • Working knowledge of RAG and at least one agent framework.
  • Successful completion of the Common FDE Foundation gate.
Key Outcomes

On completion, a participant can:

  • Frame a Retail/CPG use case and justify agent versus workflow, and single versus multi-agent, with comparative evidence.
  • Architect enterprise RAG, conversational and document-intelligence systems that meet quality, latency and cost targets.
  • Build secured tool and MCP-style integrations with identity, authorisation, idempotency and compensation.
  • Design memory, state, policy and human-approval flows appropriate to the business risk of each action.
  • Establish an evaluation strategy spanning offline, online, adversarial and regression testing as a release gate.
  • Operate an agent fleet: tracing, model routing, cost control, incident containment and rollback.
  • Own a three-month agent workstream commercially — scope, staffing, rollout, adoption and ROI.
Target Role in the Pod

Onsite-capable workstream owner. Level 2 participants set agent boundaries, own the architecture and the security posture, lead offshore execution, run the customer architecture review, and carry accountability for whether the solution is adopted and delivers measurable value.
