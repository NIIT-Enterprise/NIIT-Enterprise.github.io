Context

The role of technology leaders is evolving as organizations transition toward AI-enabled, data-driven, and platform-centric engineering environments. Program Managers are expected to go beyond delivery and team performance to make informed decisions on architecture, data and AI, engineering productivity, quality, governance, and value realization.

This creates a need for a new generation of Program Managers who can bridge business, customer, technology, and engineering teams. The program is designed to develop the mindset, technical fluency, architectural thinking, data and AI understanding, enterprise thinking, quality and platform engineering and leadership capabilities required to succeed in this evolving role.

The program will serve as a focused transition pathway for a select group of high-potential Program Managers and related technology leaders with relevant experience, motivation, and exposure to technology-led delivery. Supported by clearly defined entry criteria and competency assessments, it aims to prepare participants for Program Manager roles rather than serve as a generic upskilling intervention.

Learning Outcomes

  • Redefine the Program Manager role for the AI era: from tracking people, cost and timelines to leading technology, engineering, AI-enabled delivery and business outcomes.
  • Build the technical fluency to make sound decisions on architecture (enterprise and agentic), platforms, data and AI models, and to work as a credible partner to architects and engineers.
  • Redesign how teams build software with an AI-native SDLC, quality engineering for probabilistic systems, platform engineering and measurable engineering productivity and developer experience.
  • Lead and govern AI-enabled delivery: agents and agent management, human-in-the-loop, orchestration, AI governance, privacy, security, risk controls and escalation.
  • Think at enterprise level by connecting technology decisions to reliability, compliance, security, resilience, integration, cost and client outcomes.
  • Lead human + AI teams across generations and geographies with developer empathy, clear decision rights, delegation and sound judgement.

About StackRoute and RPS Consulting

StackRoute and RPS Consulting operate as NIIT's enterprise capability transformation arm, combining advisory, role-based tech skilling, OEM-certified training, formative review, and scaled delivery into one integrated outcome-led platform. The combined entity builds four core capabilities for enterprises: modern engineering (Full-stack, DevOps, SRE, and platform ownership enabling end-to-end engineering excellence), data and AI (data-to-decision systems and production-grade AI deployment), architecture and leadership (developing architects, technology leaders, and transformation owners with system-level thinking), and execution excellence at scale (strengthening project and program management through certifications and delivery governance). The portfolio spans the full talent lifecycle, from campus hiring through senior leadership transformation, organized by business role and career stage, and measured by workforce outcomes rather than training hours.
